LetychkaЛетучка

A teacher uploads a PDF or DOCX, or pastes text, and gets a ready online test. Students open it by link or QR code, with no registration.

Built for teachers, tutors, university departments and company training teams. Answers are checked automatically, and the teacher sees which topics students got wrong most often.

4500+users
RUinterface

PDF, DOC/DOCX, TXT up to 25 MB, or pasted text up to 100,000 characters

Automatic scoring, per-question statistics, Excel export, printable A4 sheets in two variants with an answer key
