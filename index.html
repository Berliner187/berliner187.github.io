01Main productsince 2024

2024 — now Running

LetychkaЛетучка

A teacher uploads a PDF or DOCX, or pastes text, and gets a ready online test.

Students open it by link or QR code, with no registration. Answers are checked automatically, and the teacher sees which topics students got wrong most often. Built for teachers, tutors, university departments and company training teams.

4500+users · RU interface
Input
PDF, DOC/DOCX, TXT up to 25 MB, or pasted text up to 100,000 characters
Tests
Three difficulty levels, single or multiple correct answers, shuffled options, editing of any question
Students
Link or QR code, no account needed
Results
Automatic scoring, per-question statistics, Excel export, printable A4 sheets with an answer key
